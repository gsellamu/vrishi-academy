# Arm-Raising Induction — Script & Playbook
The primary induction of the Kappasinian first session. Synthesized from Practicum 3 (101-3P, physical/literal arm-raising demonstration + deepening observations), the Practicum Scripts and PSR guides, and the First Consultation workbook. All wording original; delivery notes in parentheses.

The arm-raising is the **first (primary) induction** — it is how the client first escapes into hypnosis, and the moment their hand touches their face at the peak becomes the anchor for the "deep sleep" cue used for the rest of the session and every session after. Eye-fascination, finger-spread, and the challenges are *secondary* inductions/deepeners that ride on the association this stage installs.

---

## Delivery legend
- (slow) / (rapid) — pacing
- (voice ↓ / ↑) — pitch/volume drop or lift
- (paternal) — direct, declarative; default for Physical/literal
- (maternal) — warm, permissive; default for Emotional/inferred and challenge-adjacent moments
- (pause Ns) — silent beat
- (WAIT) — hard stop until the client responds (nod / shake / finger)
- (WATCH) — eyes on the body: which way the hand rises, breath, eyelid flutter, bicep, head drop
- (SNAP) — snap immediately before "deep sleep"
- (TOUCH) — light forehead contact at the peak (alternative to the snap; get pre-talk consent)
- (E-swap / P-keep) — soften for Emotional, keep direct for Physical

---

## 1. Why arm-raising, and what it is doing
- The whole first session is a build: presenting issue → what hypnosis is → expectations and hope → suggestibility questions → **arm-raising**. Every step pours in message units (mind + body + environment, ~60,000/day baseline); the induction pushes that load past the critical filter until the client *escapes* into hypnosis.
- The escape is not sleep and not loss of control — it is a narrow, comfortable focus. The client is conscious and in control throughout; what changes is depth and, afterward, how much they remember.
- The **peak of suggestibility** is the instant of skin contact (hand to face). At that instant you say "deep sleep" and either (SNAP) or (TOUCH) the forehead — this welds the cue word to the escape. That association is the reusable tool: in later sessions a secondary induction plus "deep sleep" drops them straight back in.
- Create a **wide range of depth** in session one on purpose — it is a dynamic tool for this session and every subsequent one. The bigger the message-unit build, the bigger the escape and the deeper the range available.

## 2. Setup and mechanics (paternal, precise)
- Client upright, feet flat, back to the chair; the working arm and hand resting ON the table; other hand on the thigh. Glasses off, no gum. (guide them into position — you direct, they follow; this is itself an early compliance check)
- Pre-check the movement: ask them to raise the hand to the forehead once (you are checking the mechanics AND inferring the arc the hand will travel). (this quietly rehearses the target)
- Take away verbal responses: from here answers are non-verbal — nod / shake / look at me. (removing speech adds overload → more message units)
- Capture the arm: "look from the elbow to the fingertips, take a good picture, close the eyes; when you can see that arm and hand clearly in your mind, nod." (WAIT) (connects them to their own body; also the last visualization check)
- The last suggestibility question must be "can you visualize?" — if no, downshift to "imagine" or "conceptualize" so no one fails. (WAIT) (a client who cannot picture can still imagine; never leave them unable to comply)

---

## 3. Physiological changes (convincers; slowing; WATCH continuously)
- Frame first: "I will notice these changes before you do — when YOU become aware of each one, nod." (moves them out of autonomic and into consciously noticing breath, etc. — more overload, and each noticed change is a convincer)
- Breathing deepens (more oxygen) — nod (WAIT) (WATCH the chest; time your line to a real inhale)
- Lips / mouth / throat dry, urge to swallow — natural and normal — nod (WAIT) (WATCH the throat)
- Eye flutter beneath the lids — "that is the first stage of hypnosis" (voice ↓) (declare it; no WAIT)

---

## 4. Conversion — Literal / Physical lane (P-keep; paternal; rhythmic, driving)
(WATCH the hand the entire time; pace your words to what it is actually doing, never ahead of it)

- Attention path, direct: elbow pressing into the table → forearm → **wrist** → hand → fingertips. (focus the wrist — a specific physical area; the Physical Suggestible's hand tends to rise led by the wrist)
- Direct declarations from the very start: "lift and rise, lighter and lighter" — light as a feather, then lighter than that (these are direct suggestions; repeat in waves synced to inhales)
- Name the twitches as fact: small pulling and jerking motions are the start (they WILL appear; naming them as they happen is the convincer loop)
- Misdirection interleave (handles the message-unit overload): the OTHER hand heavier on the leg... feet pressing the floor... back on the chair... rhythm of the breath — rotate these; **4th nod** when they feel the weight difference. (WAIT) (rotation keeps load high without repetition fatigue)
- Ownership frame: "you're creating that sensation" — their skill, not yours.
- Once the hand actually moves off the surface, escalate to directive language: "twist and turn" — palm and fingertips turning inward, upward and inward. (twisting/turning makes it mechanically easier for the hand to reach the forehead)
- **Head-to-hand close** (P3): begin suggesting the head grows heavy and drops down to meet the rising hand — closing the gap from both ends. (WATCH: steer the hand toward the forehead, NOT the eyes — avoid fingers poking into eyes; guide to brow/temple)
- Bicep cue: "the instant you feel the bicep tighten, nod." (WAIT)
- Keep the weave going until the hand is ~six inches from the face, then the contact line.

## 5. Conversion — Inferred / Emotional lane (E-swap; maternal; softer, spacious)
- Same attention path, permissive: "let your attention drift..." "you might imagine the hand becoming lighter..."
- Imagery carries the lift: many powerful helium balloons tied around the wrist → a floating feeling. (the Emotional Suggestible's hand tends to rise straighter out, not wrist-led — expect and allow that)
- Control-preserving language throughout: "you're creating that — a greater sense of control," "because you choose to allow it," "giving yourself permission," "peaceful," "safe." (E-clients must feel in control or they resist)
- Same misdirection rotation, softer listing quality; weight-difference nod. (WAIT)
- Double-bind on approach: "I wonder whether it feels more like the hand drawn to the face, or the face to the hand, or both drawn together — I'll let you decide." (either answer = deeper compliance)
- Head-to-hand close as above, gently. (still steer to brow, not eyes)

## 6. Peak, escape, and the anchor (both lanes)
- "When the hand comes all the way in to touch the face — the moment you make skin contact — nod, and you reach your peak of suggestibility and enter the hypnotic sleep." (WAIT for contact + nod)
- (SNAP or TOUCH forehead) "Deep sleep." (voice ↓↓, strong) — this instant is the anchor being set.
- **PHS immediately**, full anatomy: cue words ("deep sleep") + "for the purpose of hypnosis" + "with your permission" + "quickly, calmly, and deeply" (or "to this state or even deeper") + "and the physical body relaxes." (measured, formal — this is the contract)
- If the hand stalls before contact: keep weaving light-hand/heavy-hand/breath, add the head-drop, and give it time; do not force. If it never quite lands, convert on the closest approach ("as it comes in, the eyes close, deep sleep") — the escape still holds.

## 7. The depth ladder (what you are watching for after the escape)
Depth shows in the eyes under the lids. Name it silently; it tells you how far you can take the work.
| Depth | Eye sign under the lids | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Hypnoidal | up-and-down, quick (REM-like) | first depth reached |
| Cataleptic | side-to-side, more fluid, slower | middle depth |
| Somnambulism | roll up; whites may show (like a sleeping child) | deepest; three stages below |

**Somnambulism stages** carry increasing *post-session amnesia* (the client is fully conscious and in control during; the forgetting happens afterward, like fading dream-recall):
- Stage 1 ≈ 30% amnesia · Stage 2 ≈ 60% · Stage 3 ≈ 80%+
- Everyone can reach somnambulism; a true Somnambulist in suggestibility (≈50/50) gets there most easily. An Emotional Suggestible may be *deep* yet report feeling *light* (lower ego sensation) — trust the eye signs over their self-report.
- Go as deep as the goal needs: light for most self-improvement; very deep for e.g. hypno-anaesthesia (dental work in place of Novocaine). When you need depth, the wide range you built here is what lets you get there.

## 8. Deepeners after the escape (stack 2-3; PHS after each; 6-8 PHS reps total across the session)
Order note: a simple **5→0 countdown** is a good first deepener straight off the conversion. Then choose from:
- **Reactional** — eyes open on cue, close on "deep sleep," twice as deep; ×4. Moving the client to a recliner is a longer-form reactional deepener (do it here, narrating calm). (each lighten-then-drop nets a deeper return)
- **Arm rigidity** — tension floods the outstretched arm, steel bar shoulder-to-wrist, 5→0 building stiffness, then the try-to-bend paradox. **If in person and the arm does not actually feel stiff, do NOT challenge them to bend it — just have them release all the tension.** (never stage a challenge you can see will fail)
- **Eye fascination** — spot above eye level; **the key is to take away head movement so the eyes naturally close as the object/gaze lowers.** Doubles as a secondary induction. (see the eye-fascination playbook for the full two-lane script)
- **Heavy/Light** — palm up holds "a heavy bag of sand pressing down" (literal); other hand thumb-up "has helium balloons tied to it" (inferential). Also a suggestibility test — watch which hand responds more. Release both, deep sleep.
- **Staircase / Path** — check comfort first (finger-raise if they feel safe); ten steps down, each doubling relaxation, sensory detail. **Higher Physical Suggestible: you can add a gentle rock back and forth as they "go down the stairs."** Elder → level garden path; child → playground slide, 3-count.
- **Focus on breathing** — "inhaling relaxation, exhaling all tension, deeper with each breath."
- **Focus on sounds** — "the sounds of everyday life will only send you deeper." (inoculates against any errant room noise for the rest of the session)
- **Progressive relaxation** — usually LAST. Seated upright → start at the head, work down. Reclined → start at the feet, work up. (P responds to "relax"; E responds better to "calm.") Terminal 5→0 → deep sleep.

## 9. Laws of Suggestibility (why the repetition and voice choices work)
1. **Association** — over time the client links your voice, the room, and the process itself to the hypnotic state; "deep sleep" comes to mean that calm. (this is exactly what the peak-anchor is exploiting)
2. **Repetition** — repeating suggestions within a session and across sessions strengthens them; repeat the *ways* you hypnotize them too. (the reason "deep sleep" + PHS is given 6-8× in session one)
3. **Delayed action** — strong with Emotional and Intellectual Suggestibles. (a) goal tied to a future date manifests then (a desensitized flight fear pays off on the trip); (b) inference can take a week or two to land — a Physical may recall a dream in a day or two, an Emotional in five or six.
4. **Dominance** — default maternal/lulling voice, but a paternal/dominant voice is right for challenges and for emphasis words in a suggestion. You are the expert in the room — a natural one-upmanship, never domineering.
5. **Reverse action** — given a two-part suggestion, the client follows the *stronger* part. "You can try to open your eyes, but the harder you try the more they stay shut — you cannot open them": "cannot" beats "try," so they follow "cannot" (deepener + convincer). If they do open, "Good — you passed the test!" and move on. Also used to re-associate a trigger: get them calm, challenge them to re-create the anxiety, and calm wins (you can't be anxious and calm at once) — installing calm where the trigger used to fire.

## Appendix A — E/P quick read at the arm
| Signal | Physical / Literal | Emotional / Inferred |
|---|---|---|
| Hand rises | led by the **wrist** | straighter **out** from the arm |
| Language that lands | direct: "it IS lifting" | permissive: "you might allow it to rise" |
| Needs | to FEEL hypnosis (body convincers) | to feel UNDERSTOOD (rapport, imagery, control) |
| Self-report vs depth | report ≈ actual depth | may feel light while actually deep |
| Suggestion verbs | is / will / does | might / can / allow / tendency |

## Appendix B — Fault table
| What you see | Likely cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Hand won't lift | too little load, or pace ahead of the body | more misdirection rotation + breath sync; slow down; wait |
| Hand rises but stalls mid-air | message units easing off | re-announce twitches + heavy hand + head-drop; keep the weave |
| Fingers heading for the eyes | arc not steered | guide to brow/temple; cue head-to-hand toward the forehead |
| Client speaks / opens eyes | critical mind re-engaged | quiet reset, drop voice, resume the weave; "that's fine, close them again" |
| Arm-rigidity challenge would fail (arm soft) | not actually stiff | skip the bend-challenge; just release the tension |
| Emotional client reports "nothing" | low ego sensation, not low depth | read the eyes; proceed on the eye-signs, reassure |

## Appendix C — PHS anatomy (say it as one unit, 6-8× per first session)
cue words → "for the purpose of hypnosis" → "with your permission" → "quickly, calmly, and deeply" (or "to this state or even deeper") → "and the physical body relaxes."
Placement: at the peak, and after every deepener in the first session. This is what makes the second-session "deep sleep" drop them straight in.

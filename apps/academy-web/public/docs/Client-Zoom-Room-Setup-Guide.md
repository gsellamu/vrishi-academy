# Client Zoom Room Setup Guide

**VRishi Hypnotherapy -- Community Service Program**

Thank you for scheduling your hypnotherapy session. To ensure the best possible experience, please prepare your environment using the guidelines below.

---

## Before Your Session

### 1. Choose a Private, Quiet Space

- Select a room where you will not be interrupted for the full session (60--90 minutes).
- Close the door and let others in your household know you are unavailable.
- Turn off or silence your phone and any other devices that may create notifications.
- Pets should be in another room if possible.

### 2. Set Up a Comfortable Seat

- Use a recliner or comfortable chair where you can relax fully.
- Avoid your bed -- staying seated helps maintain the right level of focus.
- Have a pillow or blanket nearby if it helps you feel comfortable.
- Your feet should rest flat on the floor or on a footrest.

### 3. Lighting

- Soft, indirect lighting is ideal -- avoid harsh overhead lights.
- Close curtains or blinds to reduce outside distractions and glare on your screen.
- Your face should still be visible to the practitioner on camera.

### 4. Audio

- Use headphones or earbuds for the best audio quality and privacy.
- If using speakers, ensure the volume is clear and comfortable.
- Test your microphone before the session -- the practitioner needs to hear you clearly.

### 5. Camera Position

- Position your camera at eye level so the practitioner can see your face and upper body.
- This allows the practitioner to observe your responses during the session.
- A stable surface or stand is preferred over holding your device.

### 6. Internet Connection

- A wired Ethernet connection is preferred for stability.
- If using Wi-Fi, sit close to your router and minimize other devices on the network.
- Close unnecessary browser tabs and applications.

---

## Joining the Session

1. You will receive a Zoom meeting link via email or text before your appointment.
2. Click the link a few minutes early to test your audio and video.
3. You will be placed in a waiting room -- the practitioner will admit you at the scheduled time.
4. Have a glass of water nearby.

---

## During the Session

- Keep your camera on throughout the session.
- You may close your eyes when instructed -- this is a normal part of the process.
- There is no need to take notes. Just listen and follow along.
- You will remain aware and in control at all times.

---

## After the Session

- Take a few minutes to sit quietly before resuming your normal activities.
- Drink water and avoid strenuous activity for 30 minutes.
- You may feel deeply relaxed -- this is normal and positive.
- If you received a self-hypnosis recording, listen to it daily as instructed.

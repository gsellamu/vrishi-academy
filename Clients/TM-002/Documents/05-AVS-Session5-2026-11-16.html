What We Worked On Today

Today's session focused on the feelings of shame and unfamiliarity that have come up as you've been making positive changes. You described feeling like you're "not acting like yourself" even though the changes are good, and a sense that being more open or vulnerable could be risky.

We explored why your deeper mind creates this pull-back response — it's your subconscious running a security check on the new patterns, because for most of your life, staying small and guarded was the safest strategy. That program served you well when you needed it. Now, it's time for an upgrade.

What We Did in Trance

  • Safety Sphere: We built an upgraded protective space — a transparent dome or sphere around you. You can see out, interact with the world, experience everything — but nothing harmful can penetrate. This is your secure base.
  • Shame Dissolution: We located the feeling of shame in your body, gave it a color and shape, acknowledged its protective purpose ("you kept me small because small was safe"), and released it with gratitude. It's retired now — not erased, but no longer needed.
  • Two Versions of You: We visualized your old self (the one always scanning, always guarded) and your new self (the one who felt settled at the group, who is managing feelings). The old self handed his experience to the new self — not as armor, but as wisdom. They integrated.
  • Future Self: You walked through a door into 6 months from now and experienced yourself living as this new, integrated version. We locked this in with your hand on your heart and your personal anchor word.

Key Insights

  • The shame you feel about changing is actually evidence that real change has occurred. If nothing had shifted, there would be nothing to pull back from.
  • Your old identity (guarded, vigilant, small) is not your enemy — it's your history. It kept you alive. Now it can retire with honor.
  • The new you is not a performance. It's what was always underneath, before the survival program installed a different operating system.
  • Vulnerability within appropriate boundaries (the "protocol" of each relationship) is safe. You get to choose how much you share, with whom, and when.

Between-Session Practice

Daily Anchor (2 min before sleep): Place your hand on your heart. Take a deep breath. Say your anchor word silently. Let your subconscious reinforce the new version of you. This is the most important practice — repetition is how the deeper mind learns.
Shame Noticing: When the "this isn't me" feeling shows up, don't fight it. Notice it. Thank it ("you kept me safe by keeping me small"). Then gently redirect: "I don't need to be small anymore." No force — just gentle recognition.
Listen to your S4 recording + "The Room You Build" movie: Each listen deepens the integration. Your subconscious responds to repetition.
Journal one moment per day where the new self showed up naturally — not forced, not performed. Just present. Send to Jeeth before the next session.

Step 2: Primary Wound Identification — Lisa's Method 10 min
"There are three primary wounds underneath everything else. These are patterns, not diagnoses. I am going to describe each one. Just notice which one lands. Which one your body reacts to."
Abandonment: "The people who were supposed to keep you safe could not be present. The message: I was not important enough. I was not worth being present for. The care and protection that should have been there was missing. You felt alone in moments when you should not have been alone."
Betrayal: "The people who were supposed to protect you hurt you instead. Through words, actions, choices. The message: the people closest to me are dangerous. If they can hurt me, anyone can."
Shame: "You were not who your parents or family wanted you to be. Not the right kind of kid. The message: there is something fundamentally wrong with who I am."
"Which of these feels most true for you? Which one lands the hardest? It can be more than one."
PAUSE. Give genuine time. Watch face and body. Do not fill the silence. His body answers before his words.
Clinical expectation: likely BETRAYAL (family hurt him, mother) + ABANDONMENT (felt alone, not protected). Possibly SHAME. Do NOT lead him. Let him choose.

John Melton (Inner Child + Expectations)

Expectations check: The pre-talk (Phase 3) correctly frames what inner child work IS and IS NOT. Not regression. Not reliving. Observing from adult safety. This manages expectations before trance.

George Kappas (Reprogramming vs. Story)

Assessment: The therapist is walking the line correctly. Sessions 1 and 3 gathered the story. Session 2 delivered experiential programming (tribe circle, anchor). Session 4 targets the PROGRAMMING (two specific beliefs) not the story (childhood narrative). This is the right balance.

Concern: Don't let the client loop in the childhood narrative during S4. You have the story. You identified the beliefs. Now you reprogram them. The script should move from "meet the child" to "correct the belief" without extended story-dwelling. The current script handles this -- maintain discipline in delivery.

Key principle applied: "The issue is not the childhood event. It's the message units that got hardwired." The S4 script targets exactly two message units: "nobody cares" and "I'm not good." Correct.

Saman Nasir (Neuroscience-Informed)

5. THERAPIST SELF-ASSESSMENT

What's Working

  • Emotional-lane delivery is landing: Permissive, imagery-rich, client-discovers approach is producing real behavioral change. The protector energy reframe transferred to real life without explicit practice instruction -- it integrated subconsciously from the S2 trance experience.
  • Compound anchor strategy: Each session layers new associations. The anchor is becoming a multi-dimensional tool. By S4, one gesture will contain tribe + circle + workshop + child integration.
  • Session adaptation discipline: Following the client's material has been correct every time. The prepared scripts serve as safety nets and demonstrate preparation, but the real work happens in the adaptive space.
  • Documentation-as-thinking: The preparation process (scripts, runbooks, technique docs) clarifies clinical thinking before the session. Even when the script isn't delivered verbatim, the preparation makes the adaptation more precise.

What Needs Improvement

  • SMART baselines: Three deferrals is unacceptable. Collect in S4 opening. No exceptions.
  • Trance-to-talk ratio: S4 MUST deliver trance work. Two consecutive non-trance sessions risks the client (and the assessor) questioning whether this is hypnotherapy or counseling.
  • CCH pace: 9 hours toward 72 in 45 days is behind. Need to accelerate scheduling and/or add clients.
  • Post-session checklists: Several items from S2 checklist still unchecked (recording sent? tracker exported?). Need to close the loop within 24 hours of each session.

S4 Risk Assessment

Abreaction risk: MODERATE. Inner child work with a client who has developmental trauma (not single-event) carries higher abreaction risk. The client has never been in trance for reparenting work. Mitigation: safe room established first, agency language, gated Phase 10, deepen-don't-count-out protocol, exit signal reviewed.
Intellectualization risk: MODERATE. Prior psychotherapy client. May analyze rather than experience. Mitigation: if narrating, redirect to body sensation. "Where do you feel that?" not "What do you think about that?"
Incomplete processing risk: LOW-MODERATE. If only one reparenting belief is addressed (Phase 9 but not Phase 10), the session is still therapeutically valuable. One deep correction is better than two shallow ones. Phase 10 is explicitly gated.

3. Key Therapeutic Scripts

Shame Dissolution (in trance)

Now I'd like you to notice where in your body you feel that sense of shame... that feeling that comes up when you realize you're changing... when you notice you're not reacting the way you used to. Just notice where it lives. Maybe it's in your chest... your stomach... your throat. Just observe it.
Give it a color if you can. A shape. A temperature. Just observe it without trying to change it. This feeling has been with you for a long time. It had a purpose. In a world where being different meant being unsafe... shame kept you small... and small meant surviving.
But you are not in that world anymore. You are in a world where being different... being settled... being open... is not dangerous. It is actually the most natural thing you can be. So I'd like you to gently... with compassion... acknowledge that feeling. Thank it for what it did. And then... when you're ready... let it know that you don't need to be small anymore. You can let it go. Not because it was wrong... but because you've outgrown it.

Identity Update (Two Chairs)

Imagine two chairs in your safe room. In one chair sits the version of you that you've known for years. The one who is always on guard. The one who scans every room. The one who keeps people at a distance because closeness has meant pain. That version of you is real. That version kept you alive.
In the other chair... sits the version of you that's been emerging these past few weeks. The one who felt settled at the group. The one who managed the feelings. The one who is right here, right now, doing this work. That version is also real. And it is not an act. It is not a performance. It is what was always underneath... before the trauma installed a different program.
Now let the old self look at the new self. What does the old self see?... And let the new self look at the old self. What does the new self feel?... gratitude, maybe. Compassion. "You got us here. I'll take it from here."
When you're ready... let the old self hand something to the new self. Maybe it's the shield. Maybe it's the vigilance. Maybe it's just a look that says: "I trust you." And the new self receives it... not as armor... but as wisdom. "I know what danger looks like. And I also know what safety feels like. Both are mine now."

Future Self Anchor

Now imagine a door in your safe room that you haven't opened before. Behind this door is six months from now. You're going to walk through it... and when you do... you'll see yourself living this new life. Not performing it. Not acting. Living it. This is who you are when the old program is fully updated.
What do you see?... Where are you?... Who is with you?... How does your body feel?... Take a deep breath and lock this in. Hand on heart. This is you. This is real. This is not an act. This is the person you were always meant to become.

PART 4: THEORY OF MIND + EDUCATION (10-15 min)

Conscious vs Subconscious

Let me explain what is happening in your brain and why the psychotherapy helped but could not finish the job.

You have two minds. The conscious mind is the one you are using right now. It analyzes, reasons, makes decisions. It is about 12 percent of your mind's processing power. The other 88 percent is the subconscious. It runs automatically. Your heartbeat, your breathing, your emotional reactions, your habits, your beliefs about yourself. All subconscious.

When you were a kid, experiences got programmed directly into that 88 percent. No filter. No analysis. Just raw encoding. The beliefs that got hardwired, things like "I am not safe," "I cannot trust people," "I am not enough," those are not thoughts you chose. They are programs that were installed by experience.

Psychotherapy works with the 12 percent. It gave you conscious understanding. You know what happened. You know it was not your fault. You know intellectually that you are safe now. But the 88 percent did not get the update. It is still running the old programming. That is why the anxiety still runs the show. That is why the hypervigilance is still there. Your conscious mind knows better. Your subconscious has not caught up.

What we do in hypnotherapy is bypass the conscious mind temporarily and speak directly to the 88 percent. We update the programming at the source. Not by understanding it, but by giving the subconscious a new experience that overwrites the old pattern.

Fight-Flight-Freeze (Nervous System)

Here is another way to think about it. Your nervous system has a threat detection system. It was designed to protect you. When you were a kid and things were not safe, that system went on high alert. It learned to be hypervigilant because hypervigilance kept you safe.

The problem is, it never turned off. You are 22 now. You are safe. But the alarm system is still running at the same level it was at when you were five. That is not a character flaw. That is a survival program that did its job and does not know it can stand down.

Part of what we are going to do is teach that system that it is safe to turn down the volume. Not turn it off. You still need it. But right now it is at a ten. We want to bring it to a two or a three. Appropriate vigilance, not hypervigilance.

AI Disclosure

One more thing before we begin the relaxation. I want to be transparent about my preparation process. I use AI-assisted tools to research best practices, prepare session materials, and track clinical notes. All the therapeutic decisions, what techniques to use, how to adapt them for you, those are mine. The AI is a preparation tool, not a therapist. I mention this because transparency matters to me.

PART 4: THEORY OF MIND + EDUCATION (10-15 min)

Conscious vs Subconscious

Let me explain how this process works and how it connects to what you have already experienced in therapy.

You have two processing systems. The conscious mind is what you are using right now. It analyzes, reasons, makes decisions. It handles about 12 percent of your mind's total processing. The other 88 percent is the subconscious. It runs automatically. Your heartbeat, your breathing, your emotional reactions, your habits, your core beliefs about yourself and the world. All subconscious.

When you were a kid, experiences got encoded directly into that 88 percent. No filter. No adult analysis. Just raw emotional encoding. The beliefs that were formed, things like "I am not safe" or "I cannot trust people close to me" or "I have to stay on guard," those were not thoughts you chose. They were patterns your mind created to protect you. And they did protect you. They kept you alert when you needed to be alert.

The therapy you have done already made real progress. You said so yourself. That work gave you understanding, coping tools, and the ability to talk about what happened. Those are genuine gains, and we are going to build on them.

What you are describing now, the reactions that are still there despite that understanding, those often involve patterns that were encoded very early, before you had words for them. They can be harder to shift through conversation alone because they do not live in the conversational part of the brain. They live in the experiential part.

What we do in hypnotherapy is work with a deeply relaxed state where your mind is more open to experiencing things differently. Not just understanding a pattern, but feeling it shift. We are adding an experiential layer to the insight you already have.

The Nervous System (fight-flight-freeze)

Here is another way to think about it. Your nervous system has a threat detection system. It was designed to protect you. When you were a kid and things were not safe, that system went on high alert. It learned to be hypervigilant because hypervigilance kept you safe. That was intelligent adaptation. Your brain did exactly what it was supposed to do.

The challenge is that it never fully recalibrated. You are 22 now. Your circumstances have changed. But the detection system is still running at the sensitivity level it was set to years ago. That is not a character flaw. That is not weakness. That is a protective program that did its job and has not received the update that the situation has changed.

Part of what we are going to do is help that system learn that it can adjust the sensitivity. Not turn off. You still need it. You need appropriate awareness of your environment. But right now the dial is set very high. We want to bring it to a level that matches your actual circumstances. Appropriate awareness, not hypervigilance.

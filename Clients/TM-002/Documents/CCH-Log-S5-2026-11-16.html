3. What hypnotic techniques and suggestions did you use in this session?

Extensive pre-talk psychoeducation (25 min) followed by full hypnotherapy trance session with induction, deepening, and three therapeutic interventions:

  • Psychoeducation — Homeostasis + Boundaries (25 min, pre-trance): Explained Kappas homeostasis principle: the 88% (subconscious) prefers known patterns, even negative ones, over unknown patterns, even positive ones. Used water cup metaphor (new water displaces old), new leaves/old leaves metaphor (old patterns fall off as new grow). Introduced protocol-based relationship framework: each relationship (doctor-patient, friend, social group) has a scope and boundaries. Vulnerability is context-dependent, not all-or-nothing. Addressed identity vs. experience: "what happened to you is not your identity." True self is beneath accumulated layers of DNA, environment, and labels.
  • Emotional-Lane Arm-Raising Induction (8 min): Breathing lead-in (3 cycles), awareness to right arm, helium balloon lifting metaphor, magnetic forehead pull, permissive palm twist toward face, PHS "Deep Sleep" keyword on touch. Consistent with S2/S4 induction for repetition = association = deeper response.
  • 10-Step Staircase Deepener + Safety Sphere Construction (8 min): Descended 10 steps into subconscious state. Upgraded S4 safe room into a transparent safety sphere/dome (like Amazon Spheres / Earth's magnetic field). Client can see out, interact with the world, experience everything — but nothing harmful penetrates. Inner child integration from S4 reactivated within the sphere. Comfortable sofa, full sensory construction.
  • Somatic Shame Dissolution (10 min): Invited the shame/vulnerability feeling to come forward. Located it in the body (client identified specific area via IMS nod). Assigned color and shape. Traced origin to childhood: "staying small was a survival strategy" because being noticed/different/visible was not safe. Acknowledged the feeling's protective purpose with gratitude. Released with permission: "retired, not erased." PHS: "that old program of staying small can now complete its mission. It did what it was designed to do. Safely and comfortably, it can step aside."
  • Two-Chairs Identity Dialogue (10 min): Two chairs facing each other in the safe sphere. Left chair: old self (scans every room, keeps three steps ahead looking for the exit, shoulders tight even when nothing is wrong, watches others connect through glass). Right chair: new self (felt settled at group, managed feelings, reached out to therapist because he was growing, not falling apart). Facilitated mutual observation. Old self handed shield/vigilance to new self — not as armor but as wisdom ("I did my part, you can take it from here"). Guided optional merge: "not losing either one, gaining both." PHS: "this integration can settle deeply into your subconscious mind."
  • Future Self Anchor (5 min): Door in safe sphere leading to 6 months in the future. Client walked through, experienced life with new integrated identity fully settled. Noticed how future self carries himself (shoulders, breath, way of looking at people). Locked with kinesthetic anchor: hand on heart + deep breath + personal anchor word (collected during pre-talk). PHS: "each time you place your hand on your heart and take a deep breath, this feeling returns."
  • Post-Hypnotic Consolidation + Count-Out (4 min): Identity update continues during sleep, shame dissolves daily, "this is me" grows stronger. 1-5 count-out with explicit closure of subconscious door. Client emerged calm and reported the session was "awesome."

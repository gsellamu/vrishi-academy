GROWTH AREAS (Honest Assessment)

The transcript does not mention collecting SMART baselines (the six 1-10 scales). This is the fourth session in a row they have been deferred. The CCH log says "non-negotiable" for every session since S1. The CSP assessor flagged this in the clinical review.

3. Previewing Future Modalities During Deep Work

You explained cord cutting and cord realignment during this session. While client education is valuable, there is a clinical argument against previewing future techniques during or immediately after deep inner child work:

  • It can create anticipatory anxiety ("cord cutting sounds intense -- am I ready for that?")
  • It dilutes the present session's impact by shifting attention to what's next rather than what just happened
  • It gives the conscious mind something to intellectualize, which can interfere with subconscious processing

Fix: Save technique previews for the CHECK-IN portion of the NEXT session, not the debrief of the current one. After inner child work, the debrief should be: "How do you feel?" + homework + safety. Period. Let the work settle. You said "don't dilute the feeling" -- then don't dilute it with a mini-lecture on cord cutting.

4. Reframing Fatigue -- Did You Explore It Deeply Enough?

The client reported that the protector energy reframe lasted ~1.5 weeks then felt "forced and numbing." You correctly identified this as evidence that behavioral tools cannot reach somatic beliefs. But did you explore the specific moment it shifted? What was happening in his life when the reframe stopped working? Was there a specific trigger that broke through?

Why this matters: The 1.5-week fade rate is a data point. If you track this after S4's inner child work, you can measure whether the deeper intervention extends the durability. If the integration holds for 3+ weeks, that is quantitative evidence of subconscious-level change. If it fades at 1.5 weeks again, the somatic reprogramming did not hold and you need a different approach.

Fix: In the post-session email or at S5 check-in, explicitly ask: "How long did the 'more complete' feeling last? Is it still there? If it faded, when exactly did you notice the shift?" Track the fade rate across sessions as a clinical metric.

5. Trance Depth Verification

The transcript does not mention any formal trance depth verification (e.g., finger-spread PHS verify, arm rigidity challenge, or depth self-report). You used IMS for the protective part stand-down, which is good. But you did not establish a depth baseline before entering the inner child work.

Why this matters: If the client was in light trance (alpha) rather than deep trance (theta), the inner child work may have been more imaginal/conscious than truly subconscious. The IMS finger lift could occur at light trance. Without depth verification, you cannot calibrate the suggestions appropriately.

Fix: After the staircase deepener and before the Observer Canvas, add a quick depth check: "On a scale of 0 to 10, where 10 is the deepest relaxation you have ever felt, where are you right now?" Or use arm catalepsy. This takes 15 seconds and gives you critical calibration data.

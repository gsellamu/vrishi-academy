Critical Screening (DO BEFORE ANY INDUCTION)

Ask directly. Do not skip any of these:
  1. Active psychiatric care? "Are you currently seeing a psychiatrist, psychologist, or therapist?" — If yes, get name/details. Coordinate, do not conflict.
  2. Medications? "Are you currently taking any medications — anti-anxiety, anti-depressant, anti-psychotic, or anything else?" — If anti-psychotic or active psychosis: REFER. If anti-anxiety/anti-depressant: proceed with awareness.
  3. Suicidal ideation? "Have you ever had thoughts of harming yourself or ending your life?" — If active: STOP. Refer to 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline. Do not proceed with hypnosis.
  4. Trauma details? "You mentioned experiences from childhood. Without needing to share details today, can you tell me — was this a single event or ongoing? Were you ever physically harmed?" — Scope the trauma WITHOUT forcing disclosure. Session 1 is assessment, not processing.
  5. Triggers? "Is there anything I should be careful about — words, topics, or situations that tend to trigger strong reactions for you?" — Know before you go into trance.
  6. Panic attacks? "Do you experience panic attacks? How frequently?" — If yes, adjust induction (avoid claustrophobic imagery, use open-space visualizations).

Red Flags During Session

  • Abreaction: If client becomes visibly distressed during trance — deepen, guide to safe space, use grounding language. Do NOT count out suddenly.
  • Dissociation: If client goes blank/unresponsive — ground with physical cues ("feel your feet on the floor, feel the chair"). Bring back slowly.
  • Disclosure of abuse: If client discloses abuse of a minor or elder — mandatory reporting obligation. Document and follow CA reporting procedures.
  • Suicidal ideation disclosed: Stop session. Provide 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline. Do not continue hypnosis. Refer to licensed MHP.
  • Active psychosis signs: Disordered thinking, hallucinations, paranoid ideation — refer immediately. Hypnosis contraindicated.

Critical Screening (6 Mandatory Questions)

  1. Active care? Therapist, psychologist, psychiatrist? → If yes: coordinate. Get details.
  2. Medications? Anti-anxiety, anti-depressant, anti-psychotic? → If anti-psychotic: STOP, REFER.
  3. Safety? "Have you ever had thoughts of harming yourself?" → If active: STOP. 988. Refer.
  4. Trauma scope? Single vs ongoing? Physical vs emotional? → Scope only, no forced disclosure.
  5. Triggers? Words, topics, imagery to avoid? → Write down. Refer before every future session.
  6. Panic/dissociation? Panic attacks? Watching yourself from outside? → Adjust induction if yes.

EP-Branched Induction Strategy

If Physical (50%+)

  • Arm-raise: power words, elbow-press, magnetic pull, breathing tie-in
  • Direct language: "IS rising", "IS relaxing"
  • Deepeners: hand-forehead + reactional + heavy/light (optional)
  • Kinesthetic anchors: weight, pressure, temperature

If Emotional (50%+)

  • Arm-raise: inferential, misdirection, "you may notice", "I wonder"
  • Permissive: "you may notice", "allow", "at your own pace"
  • Deepeners: reactional only (skip hand-forehead, heavy/light)
  • Emotional anchors: safety, warmth, permission
TRAUMA RULES: No regression. No "go back to." Permission language throughout. Exit signal (right index finger). If abreaction: deepen, guide to safe space, do NOT count out abruptly. Safe space = NEW room (not childhood). Gentle count out. PHS includes "safely and comfortably."

Clinical Reminders

Melton

  • Check expectations: "What do you think hypnotherapy will do?"
  • Focus on BEHAVIOR: "What specific behavior do you want to change?"
  • Re-parenting foundations laid via safe space (becomes THE ROOM in S2)

Kappas

  • Visit past briefly. Recode. Move forward. Don't loop.
  • Preparation of Critical Mind: What's the problem? Why? What needs to change? How?
  • "Building pillars" — cognitive supports that open Critical Mind for suggestions.

Nasir (Neuroscience)

  • Limbic system holds survival maps — insight alone can't reach it
  • Amygdala (threat detection), hippocampus (memory context), vagus nerve (safety state)
  • Hypnotherapy adds experiential layer to psychotherapy insight

Evidence-Based Safeguards

  • Don't dismiss prior psychotherapy — build on it
  • EP informs style, does NOT determine trauma readiness
  • Images in trance may be symbolic, not factual
  • Agency language: "you can evaluate", "you decide"

Red Flags During Session

  • Abreaction: Visible distress in trance → deepen, guide to safe space, grounding language. Do NOT count out abruptly.
  • Dissociation: Blank stare, unresponsive → ground with "feel your feet, feel the chair." Bring back slowly.
  • Abuse disclosure: If minor/elder abuse disclosed → mandatory reporting. Document. Follow CA procedures.
  • Suicidal ideation: Stop session. 988 Lifeline. Refer to licensed MHP. Do not continue hypnosis.
  • Psychosis signs: Disordered thinking, hallucinations → refer immediately. Hypnosis contraindicated.

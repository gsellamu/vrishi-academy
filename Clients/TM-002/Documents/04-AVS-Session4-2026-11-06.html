What We Covered

This was our first deep inner child session — the work we have been building toward across all four sessions. Here is what we accomplished:
  1. We identified the specific root cause: Your discomfort in group settings does not come from the people in the group. It comes from emotional mapping — your subconscious is replaying an old relationship pattern. Specifically, your uncle was both supportive and physically frightening, which created a fused emotional response: fear and admiration mixed together. That pattern now activates whenever anyone gets close. Your conscious mind knows the people around you are safe. Your subconscious insists the situation mirrors past harm. That conflict is what creates the anxiety.
  2. We addressed why the reframing was fading: The protector energy and helper reframe from our earlier sessions worked for about a week and a half before feeling forced. This is normal. Beliefs formed over long periods become neurologically cemented. Conscious reframing can catch the pattern but cannot erase it — because the belief is stored in your body (your nervous system, your cells), not just in your thoughts. To reach those deeper patterns, we need to work in a hypnotic state, which is exactly what we did tonight.
  3. We performed inner child work under hypnosis: I guided you into a deeply relaxed state using the same techniques from Session 2 (breathing, affirmations, countdown, staircase). From that deep state, we did several things:
    • You externalized your negative feelings onto a screen in a private screening room — draining the color, shrinking the images, until they became meaningless pixelated symbols with no power over you
    • You located where in your body the abandonment feeling physically lives, and used that sensation as a compass to travel back to its origin
    • Your adult self moved through the mist of the past and met a younger version of you — the child who formed two core beliefs: “I am not worth being here for” and “I must be causing the anger”
    • Your adult self corrected those beliefs directly: the events were not the child’s fault. The child was pure and innocent. The guilt and shame are not his to carry.
    • We acknowledged and thanked the protective part of you — the firewall and watchtower that kept you safe for years — and communicated that the adult is now in charge of protection. Your right index finger lifted on its own to signal the subconscious agreed.
    • You built a safe room, brought the child into it, and the two of you integrated — embracing, holding hands, becoming one. You nodded to confirm it felt complete.

Key Concepts from This Session

Emotional mapping: Your subconscious maps present-day social situations onto childhood experiences with your uncle. The people are different, but the pattern recognition system treats them the same. This is not a flaw — it is your nervous system doing what it was trained to do. Tonight we began retraining it.
Somatic storage: The beliefs “I am not worth staying for” and “I must be causing the anger” are stored in your body, not just your mind. That is why thinking-based tools catch the pattern but cannot erase it. The trance state accesses where the belief actually lives.
Defensive strategy honored: Your withdrawal, distrust, and isolation were not character flaws. They were a rational survival mechanism built when no other options existed. We did not fight that system tonight — we thanked it and showed it that the adult is now capable of taking over.
Integration: The child and the adult are no longer separated. The child’s sensitivity, curiosity, and capacity for feeling are gifts. The adult’s strength and evidence are the protection the child always needed. Both exist in you now — together, not at war.

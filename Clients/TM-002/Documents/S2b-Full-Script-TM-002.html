SESSION 2b OVERVIEW

Session 2b is a Circle Therapy desensitization session. It builds directly on the S2a tribe circle, thumb-forefinger anchor, rescuer-helper identity reframe, and PHS installation (Deep Sleep). The mechanism: anxiety and deep relaxation are physiologically incompatible. In deep trance, the client stands inside a protective circle of golden light (linked to the S2a tribe energy). Specific triggers are introduced OUTSIDE the circle at graduated intensities. Each time the fear response fires, it collides with the entrenched relaxation and extinguishes. Four confrontation cycles target: (1) being disrespected at low intensity, (2) being disrespected at medium intensity, (3) vulnerability in a group setting, and (4) trusting someone close. The session ends with a compound anchor linking the circle to the existing thumb-forefinger anchor, post-hypnotic suggestions, and homework.

SESSION-WIDE CLINICAL RULES:
• Emotional lane throughout: permissive, imagery-rich. “You may notice,” “allow,” “at your own pace.” NO direct commands.
• No regression. Adult perspective always. Present-tense processing only.
• If abreaction: deepen trance, guide back inside the circle, grounding language. Do NOT count out abruptly.
• Permission language: “with your consent,” “only as comfortable,” “with your permission.”
• Exit signal: right index finger raise = immediate pause. Check in, offer to continue or stop.
• No negatives in suggestions: “I am safe” not “I am not afraid.” Positive images only.
• Record from Step 4 through Step 12 (recording notice through count out).
• Memories in trance may be symbolic, not factual. Do not confirm or dismiss uncertain material.
• Confrontation cycles are GATED: if the client does not settle from one cycle, do NOT escalate to the next. Deepen and repeat at the same level, or skip to the seal.
• If client meets PTSD criteria, refer to CPT/PE/EMDR specialist. Hypnotherapy complements, does not replace evidence-based trauma treatment.

3. What hypnotic techniques and suggestions did you use in this session?

Full hypnotherapy session with trance induction, deepening, and inner child reparenting. This is the first session in the case where sustained trance work was delivered (S1 = EFT/assessment, S2 = first trance with tribe circle, S3 = no trance/psychoeducation). The following techniques were employed:

  • Emotional-lane Induction: Breathing lead-in (3 cycles), hand-on-chest affirmations ("I am great," "I am a wonderful person," "Everybody genuinely likes me," "I am being loved"), eyes-open visual anchor ("happy face" — identity as helper/protector), permissive eye closure, "Deep Sleep" PHS keyword. Consistent with S2 induction for repetition = association.
  • Deepening: 5-0 countdown, 10-step staircase descending into the subconscious, progressive relaxation crown-to-toe with explicit instruction to release externally imposed burdens (insufficiency, incompetence, worthlessness framed as not intrinsic).
  • Observer Canvas Desensitization: Client guided to a private screening room where released negative feelings took visible shape on a large screen. Instructed to drain color (vivid emotions → fading B&W photographs → pixelated meaningless symbols). Externalization technique creating safe observational distance.
  • Somatic Identification + Affect Bridge: Client located the physical body location of the Tuesday group abandonment feeling. That bodily sensation was used as a thread/compass to trace the feeling back to its childhood origin point. Psychosomatic physiology explained: belief is stored in body tissue, not mind — why cognitive tools catch but cannot erase.
  • Mist Metaphor + Inner Child Meeting: Adult self guided through mist of the past to encounter younger self. Subconscious trusted to identify the correct age without conscious selection. Mist parted by adult's presence (not force). Two core false beliefs identified in the child: "I am not worth being here for" (abandonment → unworthiness) and "I must be causing the anger" (guilt → self-blame).
  • Inner Child Reparenting: Adult self corrected the false beliefs directly to the child: events were not the child's fault; the child was pure and innocent; guilt and shame are not his to carry. Agency language used — client discovered the words, therapist did not script them.
  • Secondary Gain Acknowledgment + Defensive Strategy Release: Child's withdrawal, distrust, and isolation reframed as rational survival mechanism (firewall/watchtower). Adult acknowledged and thanked the protective part. Subconscious asked to signal readiness for the adult to take over — confirmed by involuntary right index finger lift (IMS).
  • Safe Room Construction + Integration: Client built a safe room (walls, light, floor, temperature, sounds — all client-designed). Child and adult brought together inside. Guided to embrace, hold hands, and fully integrate. Client nodded to confirm integration felt complete. Deep breath carried integrated state into every cell.
  • Count Out: Ascent via staircase, 0-5 count. Subconscious door explicitly closed to prevent re-entry of old doubts. Client returned to full waking awareness.

  • IMS confirmation: The involuntary right index finger lift when the subconscious agreed to let the adult take over the protective role is the strongest evidence of trance engagement across all 4 sessions. The signal was unprompted and involuntary.

The inner child approach (bringing the child to a safe imaginal space rather than re-exposing to trauma) aligned with both the client's preference and the therapist's planned approach.

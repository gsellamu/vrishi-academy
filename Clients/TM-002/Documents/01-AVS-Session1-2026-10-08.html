  2. Practice the EFT tapping (2-3 times this week)
    When you notice anxiety or hypervigilance showing up, try the tapping we practiced:
    • Tap the side of your hand and say: "Even though I feel [name the feeling], I deeply and completely accept myself"
    • Tap through the points (eyebrow, side of eye, under eye, under nose, chin, collarbone, under arm, top of head)
    • With each point, acknowledge the feeling without judgment
    • Finish with a deep breath
    You do not need to do this perfectly. The act of acknowledging the feeling while tapping is what matters.
  3. Practice the deep breathing (daily, 1-2 minutes)
    The breathing we practiced at the end of session:
    • Breathe in slowly through your nose (4 counts)
    • Hold (4 counts)
    • Exhale slowly through your mouth (6-8 counts)
    • Repeat 3-5 times
    Best done in the morning or before bed. This trains your nervous system to shift from alert mode to rest mode.
  4. Notice your self-talk
    This week, pay attention to how you talk to yourself — especially when you make a mistake or feel frustrated. You do not need to change it yet. Just notice. Are you saying "you're so stupid" or "it's okay, take a deep breath, you've got this"? Which one would you say to your best friend? Just notice the difference. We will work with this in our sessions.

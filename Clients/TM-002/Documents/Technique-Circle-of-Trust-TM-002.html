CIRCLE OF TRUST: THERAPEUTIC RING VISUALIZATION

TM-002 (T.M., 22, 69% Emotional) | TRAUMA-001 | Technique Module | For S2b, S3, or later | THERAPIST ONLY

TECHNIQUE OVERVIEW

THERAPEUTIC PURPOSE: Address trust wound (betrayal/abandonment). Tristan's core pattern: people closest = most dangerous. Guard up. Hypervigilance in relationships. Fear of vulnerability. This technique creates a controlled, graded exposure to vulnerability and trust IN TRANCE, where the subconscious can rehearse what the conscious mind is afraid to try. The circle is his creation. He controls who enters. Agency throughout.
THEORETICAL BASIS:
• Kappasinian: Emotional suggestibility (69%) responds to imagery, metaphor, permissive framing. Trust is rebuilt through felt experience, not cognitive argument. The subconscious must FEEL trust before the conscious mind believes it is safe.
• Attachment theory: Disorganized attachment from betrayal by primary caregivers. The circle recreates a corrective attachment experience where the client is the secure base and others approach on HIS terms.
• Graduated exposure: Start with the safest person (sister), add progressively. Never force a ring member. Vulnerability increases in controlled increments. Each successful ring = evidence that trust did not result in harm.
• Somatic: Trust and vulnerability have body signatures (chest opening, shoulders dropping, breath deepening). By cueing these IN TRANCE while experiencing the circle, we wire "open body = safe" instead of "open body = exposed to harm."

CLINICAL RULES:
• Emotional lane: permissive, imagery, "you may notice." No direct commands.
• Client ALWAYS chooses who enters the circle. Never suggest a name. Never place his mother or abuser in the circle unless HE brings them.
• If he places an abuser in the ring: do NOT stop him. But add: "You decide how close. You decide how long. And you can remove them at any time."
• Exit signal: right index finger = immediate pause.
• If abreaction: deepen, return to center of circle (not safe room), ground with circle energy.
• The circle is NOT group therapy. It is a visualization of trust relationships. No one in the circle speaks unless the client initiates it.
• "Being seen" is the therapeutic mechanism. Vulnerability = allowing someone to see you without armor.

PHASE A: SAFE SPACE GROUNDING (2 min)

[Client should already be in trance and in their safe room from earlier deepening work. If this is a standalone module, induce and deepen first, then bring them to the safe room.]

You are in your room. Your safe space. Feel it around you. The walls you built. The light you chose. The temperature. The sounds. Everything here is yours. And you are completely safe.

Feel your feet on the floor of this room. Feel the ground beneath you. Solid. Stable. You are anchored here.

And feel the safety in your body. In your [body location from S2a]. That warmth. That steadiness. It is still there. It has not moved. You are grounded. You are here. And from this grounded place, we are going to do something new.

PHASE B: BUILD THE EMPTY CIRCLE (3 min)

I want you to imagine that the floor of your room begins to change. Right where you are standing, a circle begins to form. A ring. Drawn on the floor around you. It might be a circle of light. Or a line of warmth. Or a boundary you can feel but not see. Whatever form it takes, it is clear: you are at the center of a circle.

This circle is about the size of a small room. Maybe ten feet across. Maybe fifteen. Big enough to hold several people, but small enough that anyone standing in it is close to you. Close enough to see your face. Close enough to be seen.

Right now, you are the only person in the circle. Just you. Standing at the center. And I want you to notice how that feels. To be at the center of a space that is yours. Not as a target. Not as someone being watched. But as the anchor. The center. The one who decides what happens in this space.

[PAUSE 7-10 seconds. This reframes "being at the center" from vulnerability/exposure (his trauma pattern) to authority/agency. He is not the child in the middle of chaos. He is the adult at the center of his own circle.]

The edge of this circle is a boundary. Nothing crosses it without your permission. No one enters without your invitation. You are the gatekeeper. This circle is governed by one rule: you decide who comes in, how close they stand, and how long they stay. If at any point you want someone to leave, they leave. No explanation needed. No guilt. This is your space.

PHASE C: VULNERABILITY BODY SCAN (3 min)

Now, before anyone enters, I want you to notice something about yourself. Standing here, at the center of this empty circle, with the possibility that someone might enter — what do you feel in your body?

[PAUSE 10 seconds. This is critical data. Where does the anticipation of vulnerability live in his body? Chest tightening? Shoulders rising? Jaw clenching? Stomach contracting? The body's anticipatory response to potential trust reveals the somatic signature of his wound.]

Where do you feel it? Is it in your chest? Your shoulders? Your stomach? Your throat? Just notice it. You do not have to change it. Just notice where your body reacts to the idea of someone being close enough to see you.

[RECORD the body location of vulnerability. This becomes the somatic target for the technique — by the end of the circle work, this location should show reduced tension. Compare before and after.]

That feeling — that tightness, that bracing, that guard — has been your armor for years. It is not wrong. It kept you safe. It was intelligent. But right now, in this circle, in this safe space, the armor is not needed. There is no threat here. There is no betrayal waiting. There is no yelling coming. Just you. And in a moment, people you choose.

So I am going to ask your body to do something unusual. Not drop the armor. Not throw it away. Just set it down. Right here beside you. Inside the circle. Where you can pick it back up anytime you want. You are not giving it away. You are just setting it down for a moment. To see what it feels like to stand without it.

[PAUSE 7 seconds. "Setting down the armor" rather than "removing" it preserves agency and reduces resistance. The subconscious does not feel tricked into vulnerability — it feels like it made a choice it can reverse.]

With your next breath, let the shoulders drop one inch. Just one inch. Not all the way down. Just enough to feel the difference. And let the chest open one degree. Not wide open. Just one degree more than it was. That is enough. That is vulnerability. Not exposure. Not weakness. Just one degree of openness. And it is your choice.

PHASE D: RING 1 — THE SAFEST PERSON (5 min)

Now. Standing at the center of your circle, with your shoulders down and your chest one degree more open — I want you to think of one person. The safest person in your life. The person you trust the most. It does not have to be perfect trust. It just has to be the closest thing you have to it. The person who, when you think of them, your body relaxes even a little. Who is it?

[PAUSE. Let the subconscious provide the person. Do NOT suggest his sister, his grandfather, or a friend. Let him choose. Clinical expectation: likely his sister or a close childhood friend. RECORD who he names. This is the foundation of his trust map.]

Good. Now invite them into the circle. You do not have to say it out loud. Just intend it. And they appear at the edge of the circle. Standing just inside the boundary. Facing you.

Take a moment to see them. Really see them. Their face. Their eyes. The way they stand. The expression they are wearing. What are they showing you right now? What do you see on their face?

[PAUSE 10 seconds. The subconscious will project onto this person whatever the client needs to see. It might be warmth, concern, love, patience, acceptance. Whatever appears is therapeutic data. Do not interpret. Let him describe.]

Now I want you to do something that might feel unfamiliar. I want you to let them see you. Not the version of you that has the armor on. Not the version that is performing "I am fine." The version that is standing here right now. One degree open. Shoulders down. No walls between you and this person. Just you. As you actually are.

What happens in your body when you let them see you like this?

[PAUSE 10-15 seconds. THIS IS THE KEY THERAPEUTIC MOMENT. Being seen without armor. In front of a safe person. In a protected environment. The body's response tells you everything. If it softens: the trust pathway is opening. If it tightens: the wound is deep but the exposure is still therapeutic — the fact that he is TRYING is the work.]

And now notice something. They are still there. They did not leave. They did not flinch. They did not use what they see against you. They are still standing in your circle, looking at you with [whatever he described on their face]. The openness did not cause harm. The vulnerability did not invite betrayal. It invited connection.

Let that land. Let your body register: I was seen. And I was safe.

[PAUSE 7-10 seconds. Memory reconsolidation: the subconscious is filing a new experience — vulnerability led to safety, not harm. This directly contradicts the betrayal wound pattern.]

Now let them take one step closer. Just one step. They are a little nearer now. And you can feel something between you. Not tension. Not threat. Something else. Warmth. A current. Like standing near a fire on a cold night. The warmth of being near someone who is safe.

Breathe that in. Let the warmth reach the spot in your body where the armor used to sit. Let it fill that space. Not forcing. Just allowing.

Ring 1 complete. Safest person entered, was seen, did not betray, connection felt. Body registered safety-after-vulnerability. Foundation built.

PHASE E: RING 2 — THE NEXT PERSON (4 min)

Good. Now, with [Ring 1 person] still in the circle, I want you to think of a second person. Someone else you trust. It does not have to be the same level of trust. It can be a friend. A mentor. Someone who has shown you they are safe. Even in small ways. Who comes to mind?

[PAUSE. Let him choose. RECORD the second person. This maps his trust network. If he struggles: "It does not have to be someone you trust completely. Just someone who has not given you a reason to put your guard up." If he still struggles: "It can be someone from any time in your life. Past or present."]

Invite them in. They appear at the edge of the circle. Now there are two people in your ring. And you at the center.

Notice how the energy in the circle changes with a second person. It is different from one-on-one. There is a geometry to it now. A shape. They can see each other. And they can both see you.

And here is the important part: you are letting TWO people see you without armor. Two people who are witnessing the real version of you. Not the guarded version. Not the performing version. The one-degree-open version.

What happens in your body now? Is the vulnerability harder with two? Or does it feel supported? Like the second person is not doubling the exposure — they are sharing the weight of it?

[PAUSE 10 seconds. Two possible responses: (1) more vulnerable/tighter = the trust wound is activated by groups, not just individuals. Note this for future work. (2) safer/warmer = the circle is functioning as intended — more witnesses = more support, not more danger. Both responses are therapeutically useful.]

Let both of them take one step closer. The circle gets smaller. The three of you are nearer now. And the warmth between you grows. You are not surrounded. You are held. There is a difference. Surrounded means trapped. Held means supported. These people are not closing in on you. They are closing in around you. Protecting the space. Not threatening it.

PHASE F: RING 3 — THE CHALLENGE PERSON (5 min)

CLINICAL GATE: This phase is OPTIONAL for S2b. Only proceed if Rings 1 and 2 landed well (body softened, no abreaction, ideomotor nods). If the client is at capacity, skip to Phase G and seal the two-person circle. A two-person circle is a complete therapeutic unit. Do not push for three if the body says no.

IF HE PLACES AN ABUSER: Do NOT stop him. Say: "You decide how close they stand. You decide if they speak. And you can remove them instantly. Your circle, your rules." Process whatever emerges. This is advanced work — be ready for abreaction protocol.

Now, if you are ready — and only if you are ready — there is one more invitation you can make. Someone who is harder to trust. Not unsafe. Not dangerous. But someone where the trust is still under construction. Someone you want to trust but your guard is not fully down with yet. It might be a newer friend. A colleague. Someone you are getting to know. Is there someone like that?

[PAUSE. If he names someone: proceed. If he says no or hesitates: "That is perfectly fine. Two is a strong circle. We can always expand it later." Skip to Phase G. Do not push. If he names his mother or another abuser: see CLINICAL GATE above.]

Invite them to the edge of the circle. Not inside yet. Just at the boundary. They are standing at the perimeter. Looking in. Seeing you. Seeing [Ring 1] and [Ring 2]. And waiting for your permission to enter.

Notice what happens in your body. The guard wants to come back, does it not? The shoulders want to rise. The chest wants to close. That is the old programming. That is the child's rule: new person equals unknown threat. But the adult knows something the child did not know. Not every person is the people who hurt you. New does not mean dangerous. Different does not mean unsafe.

So here is your choice. You can let them in. One step inside the boundary. Not close. Not in your face. Just one step inside. Enough to be part of the circle but far enough that you have space. Or you can leave them at the edge. Both are valid. Both are your choice. What do you want to do?

[PAUSE 10-15 seconds. Let the subconscious decide. If he lets them in: "Good. Notice how the circle adjusts. The geometry shifts. Three points of connection. And you are still safe." If he keeps them at the edge: "Good. That is honest. And that honesty IS the vulnerability. You are not pretending trust you do not feel. That is strength, not weakness."]
IF THEY ENTERED:

Three people now. Three people who can see you. The real you. And you are still standing. Your heart is still beating. Your breath is still flowing. The vulnerability did not destroy you. It connected you. Three different levels of trust. Three different relationships. All coexisting in the same space. And you at the center. Not as the target. As the anchor.

IF THEY STAYED AT THE EDGE:

And that person at the edge — they are patient. They are not pushing. They are not demanding entry. They are just present. Available. And the door is not closed. It is just not fully open yet. And that is okay. Trust is not a light switch. It is a dimmer. And you control the dial.

PHASE G: THE CIRCLE HOLDS (3 min)

Now take in the full picture. You at the center. [Ring 1] close. [Ring 2] beside them. [Ring 3 if present] at whatever distance feels right. A circle of people who see you. Who are not running. Who are not attacking. Who are simply present. With you. For you.

And I want you to feel something in this moment. Feel what the circle feels like as a whole. Not just the individuals. The circle itself. The shape of it. The energy of it. There is a warmth in the center — where you are standing — that is generated by the ring around you. Like standing in the middle of a campfire circle. The heat comes from all directions. And it meets in the middle. In you.

This feeling — this warmth at the center of a circle of people who see you and choose to stay — this is what trust feels like. Not the absence of fear. The presence of connection despite the fear. That is all trust has ever been. And right now, you are feeling it. In your body. In your chest. In your bones. It is real. It is here. And your subconscious is recording it.

[PAUSE 10-15 seconds. This is the peak state of the technique. The "felt sense" of trust-in-a-group is being encoded in deep trance. The subconscious now has a reference experience for what trust in a circle feels like somatically. This is the anchor that will fire in real-world group situations.]

Now breathe in. A full breath. Hand on heart if you want it. And say to yourself: "I am [word 1] and I am [word 2] and I like it." Feel it in the circle. Feel it with these people around you. The anchor and the circle wiring together. One breath. One feeling. One truth: I can be seen and still be safe.

Seal the Circle (PHS)

For the purpose of hypnosis and with your permission, safely and comfortably, this circle is now part of your subconscious architecture. It lives inside you. You carry it with you. Wherever you go, the circle goes. The faces may change. New people may enter as you build trust with them. Others may move closer as trust deepens. But the circle is permanent. The feeling is permanent. The truth that you can be seen and survive — that is permanent. Sealed. Protected. Growing stronger.

And the armor you set down? It is still there beside you. You can pick it up anytime. But you may notice something. It is a little lighter than it was before. A little less necessary. Not because the world became safe. Because you became strong enough to be open.

PHASE H: TRANSFER TO REAL WORLD (3 min)

Trigger-to-Circle Association

Now I want to give your subconscious one more instruction. In the days and weeks ahead, you will find yourself in moments where trust is being asked of you. A conversation that goes deeper than small talk. A friend who asks how you really are. A moment where someone reaches toward you — not to grab, not to take, but to connect.

In those moments, the old pattern will try to fire. The guard. The wall. The flinch. The scanning. But before it runs, something new will happen. The circle will activate. Not visually — you will not see the ring on the floor. But you will feel it. The warmth. The center. The sense of being anchored. And you will remember: I was seen in the circle, and I was safe. I can be seen here, too.

The old trigger — the request for vulnerability, the invitation to trust — becomes the signal that activates the circle. Not every time. Not with everyone. Your judgment is still intact. You still get to choose who enters. But the automatic wall does not go up first anymore. The circle goes up first. The warmth comes first. And THEN you decide.

Graduated Trust Post-Hypnotic

Between now and our next session, you will notice moments where the wall is a little lower than before. Not gone. Just lower. Moments where you share one sentence more than you normally would. Where you hold eye contact one second longer. Where you let someone see something real. These are not reckless moments. They are graduated moments. One degree at a time. The same one degree of openness you practiced here in the circle.

And each time you take that one degree, and the person does not betray it, the circle strengthens. Each time you are seen and you are safe, another data point enters the system. And over time — not overnight, but over time — the wall becomes optional instead of automatic. The guard becomes a choice instead of a reflex. And the circle becomes the default instead of the armor.

Identity-Level Close

Because here is the truth your subconscious already knows. You are not someone who cannot trust. You are someone who learned to be careful because careful kept you alive. But careful is not the same as closed. And closed is not the same as safe. The safest version of you is not the one behind the tallest wall. The safest version of you is the one who can open and close at will. Who can read the room and choose. Who can be vulnerable with the right people and boundaried with the wrong ones. That is strength. And that is who you are becoming.

Circle of Trust technique complete. Safe person entered, client practiced being seen, somatic vulnerability registered and survived, circle sealed as permanent subconscious architecture, trigger-to-circle association installed, graduated trust post-hypnotic delivered, identity-level reframe landed.

Purpose of Recording

During certain hypnotherapy sessions, the therapy portion (guided hypnosis, progressive relaxation, suggestions, and imagery) may be audio-recorded. The recording serves as a therapeutic tool for the client to:

  1. Replay the guided session at home to reinforce therapeutic suggestions
  2. Practice self-hypnosis using the therapist's guided audio
  3. Deepen the hypnotherapeutic effect through repetition between sessions
What is recorded: Only the therapy/hypnosis portion of the session (after induction begins). The initial review, discussion, and debrief portions are not recorded. The recording captures the therapist's guided script and instructions only.

Terms and Conditions

  1. Personal use only. The recording is provided exclusively for the client's personal therapeutic use. It may not be shared, distributed, published, posted online, or used for any commercial purpose.
  2. Intellectual property. The therapeutic scripts, techniques, and guided content in the recording are the intellectual property of VRishi Hypnotherapy. The client receives a personal-use license only.
  3. Confidentiality. The recording contains Protected Health Information (PHI). The client agrees to store it securely and not share it with unauthorized persons. Recommended: store on a password-protected device only.
  4. Delivery method. Recordings will be delivered via PocketSuite (HIPAA-compliant) or another secure method agreed upon by both parties. Recordings will not be sent via unsecured email or messaging platforms.
  5. Retention. The practitioner retains a copy of the recording in the client's secure file for the duration of treatment. The client may keep their copy indefinitely for personal use.
  6. Right to decline. The client may decline recording at any time, for any session, without affecting treatment. Simply inform the practitioner before the session begins.
  7. Right to request deletion. The client may request deletion of any or all recordings from the practitioner's files at any time. The request will be honored within 30 days.
Important: Do NOT listen to hypnotherapy recordings while driving, operating machinery, or in any situation requiring full alertness. Use recordings only while seated or lying in a safe, quiet environment.

Purpose of Recording

As part of your hypnotherapy program, certain sessions (or portions of sessions) may be audio-recorded. These recordings serve the following purposes:

  1. Client self-reinforcement: You may listen to the therapy portion of your session recording between appointments to reinforce post-hypnotic suggestions and deepen the therapeutic effect.
  2. Progress tracking: Recordings help both you and the therapist review session content and track progress over time.
  3. Clinical documentation: Recordings support accurate session notes and treatment planning.

What Will Be Recorded

  • The therapy portion only of the session (induction, deepening, suggestion work, and emerging).
  • The initial consultation, suggestibility assessment, and discussion portions are not recorded unless separately agreed.
  • Recordings are audio-only (no video) unless otherwise specified.

Terms and Conditions

  1. Ownership: The recording is created by the therapist and provided to you for personal therapeutic use only.
  2. Personal use only: You agree not to share, distribute, upload, broadcast, or publish the recording in any form (social media, messaging apps, file-sharing services, etc.).
  3. Storage: Store recordings on an encrypted or password-protected device. Do not store on unencrypted cloud services or shared devices.
  4. Deletion: Upon completion or termination of the program, the therapist will delete their copy of recordings within 30 days unless you request otherwise in writing. You may retain your personal copy.
  5. Confidentiality: Recordings contain protected health information (PHI) and are subject to HIPAA Privacy Rule (45 CFR §§160, 164) protections.
  6. Revocation: You may revoke this consent at any time by notifying the therapist in writing. Revocation does not apply to recordings already made with your consent.
  7. California law: California is a two-party consent state (Penal Code §632). This form satisfies the consent requirement for both parties.
Safety Warning — Listening to Session Recordings:
  • Never listen to recordings while driving, operating machinery, or in any situation requiring alertness. Hypnosis recordings can induce trance states.
  • Listen only in a safe, comfortable environment where you can relax fully (e.g., lying in bed before sleep).
  • If you experience any distress while listening, stop the recording and contact the therapist.
  • Do not allow others to listen to your recording — the suggestions are personalized for your suggestibility profile and may not be appropriate for others.

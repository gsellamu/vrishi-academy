Part A: SB 577 Disclosure

Under California Senate Bill 577 (effective January 1, 2003), complementary and alternative health practitioners are required to disclose the following to clients before providing services:

  1. I am not a licensed physician. I am not a licensed physician, surgeon, psychologist, psychiatrist, marriage and family therapist, clinical social worker, or any other type of licensed health care provider as defined by the State of California.
  2. Hypnotherapy services are complementary. The services I provide are classified as complementary and alternative health practices under California Business and Professions Code Section 2908. These services are not a substitute for medical diagnosis, treatment, or mental health care.
  3. My training and credentials:
    • Certified Master Hypnotist (C.MH.) — Hypnosis Motivation Institute
    • American Hypnosis Association Member #007913
    • Currently completing advanced studies in clinical hypnotherapy
  4. Nature of services: Clinical hypnotherapy for vocational and avocational self-improvement, including but not limited to: sleep improvement, stress management, confidence building, habit modification, pain comfort, and performance enhancement.
  5. No diagnosis or prescription. I do not diagnose medical or psychological conditions. I do not prescribe medications. I do not provide psychotherapy.
  6. Right to refuse or discontinue. You have the right to refuse any service, terminate any session at any time, and discontinue treatment without obligation or penalty.
  7. Referral obligation. If I determine that your presenting concern may require medical or psychological evaluation, I am ethically obligated to refer you to an appropriate licensed professional.

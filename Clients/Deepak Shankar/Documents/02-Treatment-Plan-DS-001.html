Therapeutic Approach

  • Lane: Physical suggestible (direct, literal, authoritative delivery)
  • Induction: Arm-raise, progressive relaxation, eye fascination
  • Deepening: Trophy staircase (20-step), reactional sets, heavy/light, arm rigidity
  • Suggestion style: Post-hypnotic installation with ideomotor confirmation
  • Homework: Nightly sleep ceremony (10:30 PM), 4-7-8 breathing, dream journal
  • Decision points: Session 4 (independence readiness), Session 5 (graduation readiness)

Roles and Responsibilities

  • Maintain a dream journal

  • Refrain from using alcohol as a sleep aid

Evidence Base

This protocol is informed by peer-reviewed research on hypnotherapy for sleep:
  • Cordi MJ et al. (2014). "Deepening sleep by hypnotic suggestion." Sleep, 37(6), 1143-1152.
  • Chamine I et al. (2018). "Hypnosis and upper airway involvement in obstructive sleep apnea." Sleep Medicine Reviews, 37, 45-58.
  • Lam TH et al. (2015). "Hypnotherapy for insomnia: A systematic review." Complementary Therapies in Medicine, 23(5), 719-732.
